Voice Commands

  • "Start Form Filling" - Begin the form
  • "Scan Form" - Use camera to read form
  • "Repeat" - Hear the question again
  • "Skip" - Skip current field
  • "Back" - Go to previous field
  • "Reset Form" - Clear all fields
